THE PLATFORM

Modular by design.
Adaptive by necessity.

TORSO + ARMS
Dual 7-DoF manipulation. Quick-change wrists. Tool-agnostic end-effector interface. Continuous 360° torso rotation.
SENSOR HEAD
Multi-sensor dome. Visual, thermal, LIDAR, gas detection. Hardened for particulate and chemical environments.
LOWER BODY
Dynamic morphology. Bipedal, tracked, or magnetic tripod. Task-configurable in field. Fits 24" manway clearance.
END EFFECTORS
Swappable end-effectors. Blast, grip, torque, inspect. 90-second field changeout. No tool modification required.

THE THESIS

Why not a drone?
Why not a tracked robot?
Why not fixed automation?

Because hazardous work is not homogeneous. Daedamorph reconfigures in the field — swapping limbs, scaling morphology, and adapting to the task rather than the task adapting to the robot.

// WHY NOT A DRONE?
No physical work capability in GPS-denied, turbulent interiors. Drones observe. Daedamorph intervenes. Torque a flange. Cut a seal. Replace a valve. A drone cannot.
// WHY NOT A TRACKED ROBOT?
Cannot enter 24-inch manways or generate reaction forces on open grating. Fixed geometry fails variable infrastructure. Morphology is not optional — it is the capability.
// WHY NOT FIXED AUTOMATION?
Turnaround tasks change every 2–3 years. Rebuilding vessels around fixed arms is economically and operationally unacceptable. Adaptability is the specification.
